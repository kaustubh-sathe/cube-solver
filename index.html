Rubik's Cube Solver

Scan it, solve it in ~20 moves, watch every turn.

1Scan your cube

Six faces gives the most reliable read. Five works too — the solver reconstructs the last one — but it can be ambiguous.

Shoot each face square-on and centred, filling most of the frame. Only the middle square of the photo is read.

Works on a phone browser. The page must be on https for camera access.

2Check the read

Dashed outlines are squares the detector wasn't confident about. Pick a colour, then click any square to correct it. Centres are fixed.
